# Market Feeling

Daily and weekend market sentiment analysis generated by LLMs, grounded in newsletters, S&P 500 barometer readings, prediction markets, and ticker price swings. The output is stored in the `market_feeling` table and consumed by downstream tasks like the daily predictor and the web dashboard.

## Data Model

The `market_feeling` table records each sentiment snapshot with:
- `sentiment_label` (text) – core sentiment (e.g., *Bullish*, *Cautiously Optimistic*, *Bearish*). This column was originally named `feeling`; it was renamed for clarity and to distinguish the label from the broader concept.
- `news_summary` (text) – synthesized summary of the day’s most relevant newsletter stories.
- `confidence_score` (float) – optional 0–1 confidence indicator.
- `analysis_method` (text) – distinguishes daily vs. weekend analysis.
- `created_at` (timestamptz) – automatic timestamp.

## Usage
- The **daily predictor** (`[[entities/daily-market-predictor]]`) queries the latest `sentiment_label` and `news_summary` to supply emotional context for its directional prediction prompt.
- The **web dashboard** (`[[entities/ai-feeling-card]]`) displays the most recent sentiment as a visual AI feeling card.
