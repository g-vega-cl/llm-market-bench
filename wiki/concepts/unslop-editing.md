# Unslop Editing

A structured editing skill for removing AI-generated pattern tells and injecting human voice into prose. Registered as a Claude Code skill at `.agents/skills/unslop/SKILL.md`.

## Process

1. Scan for 31 defined patterns organized into content, language, style, communication artifacts, filler, jargon, and plain-speech categories.
2. Rewrite while preserving meaning and matching intended tone.
3. Add soul — have opinions, vary rhythm, acknowledge complexity, use first person when it fits, let some mess in, and be specific.
4. Self-audit: "What makes this obviously AI generated?" Fix remaining tells.

## Key Principles

- **Plain speech wins.** Prefer "use" over "utilize", "is" over "serves as", plain words over abstract metaphor nouns like "substrate" or "vector".
- **No decorative punctuation.** Em dashes, parenthetical asides, and mid-sentence colons are AI tells. End the sentence or use a comma.
- **Say what it does, not how it feels.** A sentence that could appear unchanged in another project's docs says nothing about this one.
- **Active voice, concrete facts, one idea per sentence.** Cut adverbs, shorten dense sentences, avoid hedging.
- **Avoid formulaic structure.** Rule-of-three forcing, synonym cycling, title-case headings, boldface overuse, and faux ranges are all detectable signals.

## Sources

The unslop patterns synthesize common AI anti-patterns observed across LLM-generated prose and are formalized as a repeatable editing checklist.
