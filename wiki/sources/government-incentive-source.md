# Source: Government Incentive Tracking

Quick reference for capturing government incentives for market-moving nations.

Key details:

- **Always capture**: G7 countries + EU Commission directives
- **Sector-specific**: China (semiconductors, EVs), India (PLI), Brazil (agriculture), Saudi Arabia (Vision 2030)
- **Policy types**: legislative actions, budget/spending, subsidies/tax credits, regulatory changes, agency actions
- **Ignore**: political theater (campaign promises, manifestos), too local/minor, no funding/teeth, wrong countries
- **Importance scoring**: 8-10 (economy-wide, billions), 5-7 (sector-specific), 1-4 (narrow)
- **Hard requirement**: event names must include specific bill/act name — generic names removed by system
- **Post-analysis validation**: vague government events trigger Policy Lookup (Gemini + Google Search)
