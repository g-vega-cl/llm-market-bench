# Source: Government Incentive Tracking

Synthesized from `raw/docs/reference/government-incentive-quick-ref.md`.

## Takeaways

- **High-Impact Targeting**: Focuses on G7 and major G20 economies (China, India) where policy shifts directly move markets.
- **Specificity Requirement**: Government "memories" must name the specific bill or act (e.g., "US Farm Bill 2026"). Generic "Policy Updates" are automatically purged.
- **Scenario Planning**: Uncertain legislative outcomes require at least two scenarios (Pass/Fail) with specific trading plans for each.
- **Importance Thresholds**: Scale of 1-10; only high-impact (8+) events are prioritized for the Horizon Watch dashboard.
