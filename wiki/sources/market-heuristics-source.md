## Takeaways

- **The Munger Check**: A "Stupid Person Test" used by the contrarian agent to filter out overly-obvious or consensus-driven trades.
- **Adjacency Mapping**: Logic for finding secondary and tertiary beneficiaries of a primary trend (e.g., AI $\to$ Cooling $\to$ Copper).
- **Macro Signals**: Tracking the "Forecast-Reality Gap" and "Velocity of Money" as long-term indicators of economic health versus market sentiment.
- **Social Shifts**: Monitoring demographic changes and energy transition milestones as decade-scale structural drivers.
