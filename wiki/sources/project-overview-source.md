# Source: Project Overview

Synthesized from `raw/docs/Overview.md`.

## Takeaways

- **Multi-Agent Benchmarking**: A platform where multiple LLMs compete in a virtual market to benchmark their reasoning against the S&P 500.
- **4-Phase Lifecycle**: Standardizes the path from raw news chunks to settled trades and post-mortem lessons.
- **Machine-Auditable**: Every trade is linked back to a specific reasoning trace, and every reasoning trace is linked back to a raw news source.
- **Memory Integrity**: Uses pgvector to maintain a consistent world-view and avoid repeating past trading mistakes.
